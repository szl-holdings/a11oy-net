How a claim earns its label

    Emit

    An agent produces a claim, artifact, or action proposal.

  2. 02
    Gate

    Doctrine guards check labels, banned tokens, and overclaim patterns.

  3. 03
    Sign

    Qualifying artifacts get DSSE receipts with subject digests.

  4. 04
    Publish

    Surfaces ship with the label visible — UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.

    Verify
